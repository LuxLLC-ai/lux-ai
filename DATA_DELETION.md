# Data Deletion - Lux

Lux is a fully offline AI application. All user data — including your name, age, PIN, conversation history, and any photos shared in-conversation — is stored ONLY on your device. No data is transmitted to or stored on any server.

## How to Delete Your Data

All data deletion happens within the Lux app itself, on your device:

### Delete All Conversation History (keep account):
Open Lux → Settings → "Clear all conversations"

### Delete Your Entire Account (PIN, name, age, history):
Open Lux → Settings → "Reset this account"

### Complete Removal (delete app and all data):
Open Lux → Settings → "Uninstall Lux"
Or uninstall via your Android system settings.

## No Server-Side Data
Because Lux runs entirely on your device with no cloud component, there is no server-side data for us to delete on your behalf. Removing the app from your device removes all associated data.
