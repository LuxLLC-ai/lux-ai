# Lux — Offline AI Companion

Lux is your private AI companion. She runs completely on your device using Mistral 3B Instruct 2512, speaks with a natural British voice, listens through on-device speech recognition, and remembers your conversations — all without ever connecting to the internet.

## What Makes Lux Different

- Fully offline — works in airplane mode, hospitals, anywhere
- No data collected — ever
- No servers — nothing leaves your device
- No subscription — one time purchase
- No ads — none, ever
- Voice input and output — speak naturally, she speaks back
- Live conversation mode — real time voice interaction
- Multi user — each account completely isolated

## Privacy

Your conversations are yours. Lux has no servers to send them to. No company sees what you say. No algorithm learns from you. Your data stays on your device and nowhere else.

## Built On

Lux is built on Mistral 3B Instruct 2512, released by Mistral AI under the Apache 2.0 License. The base model weights are used as the foundation. All surrounding architecture, voice pipeline, companion behavior, and Android implementation was independently designed and built by the Clarity Project.
