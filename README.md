# Lux — Offline AI Companion

Lux is your private AI companion. She runs completely on your device using Mistral 3B Instruct 2512, speaks with a natural British voice, sees through your camera, translates 11 languages, and remembers your conversations — all without ever connecting to the internet.

## What Makes Lux Different

- Fully offline — works in airplane mode, hospitals, anywhere
- No data collected — ever
- No servers — nothing leaves your device
- No subscription — one time purchase
- No ads — none, ever
- Voice input and output — speak naturally, she speaks back
- Live conversation mode — real time voice interaction with a visual presence
- Camera vision — show her what you're looking at
- 11 languages — chat, translate, and learn natively
- Multi user — each account completely isolated
- Encrypted at rest — your conversations stay yours

## Privacy

Your conversations are yours. Lux has no servers to send them to. No company sees what you say. No algorithm learns from you. Your data stays on your device, encrypted, and nowhere else.

Put your phone in airplane mode. Lux still works completely. That is the proof.

## Languages

Lux speaks, translates, and writes in 11 languages natively: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Arabic. Select your preferred language at setup. Use her as a pocket translator anywhere in the world, fully offline.

## Capabilities

- Long conversation with persistent memory
- Voice conversation through Live Mode
- Camera vision for on-device image understanding
- Journal mode with reminders
- Recipe and cooking guidance
- Story mode
- App opener — "Lux, open my email"
- Forward slash commands for quick actions
- Knowledge pack covering 2023-2026

## What Lux Is Not

- An online assistant — she has no internet capabilities and never will
- A surveillance device dressed as a friend
- A subscription product — one purchase, yours forever
- A replacement for human connection — a complement, not a substitute

## Built On

Lux is built on Mistral 3B Instruct 2512, released by Mistral AI under the Apache 2.0 License. The base model weights are used as the foundation. All surrounding architecture, voice pipeline, vision integration, companion behavior, encryption layer, and Android implementation was independently designed and built by the Clarity Project.
