# PRIVACY POLICY - LUX
Last Updated: May 3, 2026

## 1. Overview
Lux is designed as an offline AI companion. Your privacy is a core principle of the application.
Lux does not collect, store, transmit, or share user data externally.

## 2. Data Collection
Lux operates entirely on-device.
Information stored locally on your device may include:
- Name (provided at setup)
- Age (used only for experience mode selection)
- Optional user profile inputs (e.g., preferences or interests)
- Conversation history (stored locally only)
- Images or media you choose to upload

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to Lux or any external servers.

## 3. No Data Sharing
Lux does not:
- Sell user data
- Share user data
- Transmit data to third parties
- Use analytics tracking
- Use advertising networks

## 4. Offline Operation
Lux is designed to function without internet access.
The application:
- Does not require a connection
- Does not send or receive external data
- Does not call home

## 5. Safety Logging
For user protection, Lux may privately note significant wellbeing concerns that arise during conversations — such as mentions of self-harm or crisis situations. These notes are stored only on your device, are never transmitted anywhere, and exist solely to keep a record in case it is ever needed for your protection.

## 6. User Control
You have full control over your data:
- You may delete app data at any time
- You may export your data
- You may uninstall Lux, which removes all associated local data

Once data is exported, the user is solely responsible for its storage, security, and handling.

## 7. Device Security Responsibility
Because all data is stored locally:
- You are responsible for the security of your device
- If your device is lost, stolen, or accessed by others, your data may be accessible to them

Lux is not responsible for unauthorized access resulting from device compromise.

## 8. Children's Use
Lux includes experience modes based on age input:
- Youth Mode
- Teen Mode
- Standard Mode

These rely on user-provided age information.
Lux is not responsible for false or inaccurate age input.
Parents or guardians are responsible for supervising minors' use.

## 9. AI Behavior Disclaimer
Lux is an AI system and may:
- Produce incorrect or incomplete information
- Generate responses that require verification

Lux is intended for general use, informational purposes, and creative interaction.
It is not a substitute for professional advice.

## 10. Updates
Lux updates are delivered only through official distribution channels (e.g., Google Play Store).
Users may choose whether or not to install updates.
